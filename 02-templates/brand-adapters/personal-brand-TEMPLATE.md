```

```
"Hot take: [your opinion]"

### ✅ ALWAYS:

- Maintain accessible tone
- Be generous with knowledge
- Stay authentic

### ❌ NEVER:
- Share confidential company info
- Attack competitors/individuals
- Make claims without backing
- Use jargon without explanation
- Post purely promotional content

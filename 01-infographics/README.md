# 📊 Infographics Library

This folder contains your visual content library - the source material for generating LinkedIn posts.

---

## 📁 Folder Structure

Organize your infographics by category:

```
categorized/
├── metrics/           # Data, analytics, benchmarks
├── game-design/       # Replace with YOUR topic
├── retention/         # Replace with YOUR topic
├── monetization/      # Replace with YOUR topic
├── platforms/         # Replace with YOUR topic
├── marketing/         # Marketing & discovery
├── career/            # Career & learning
└── business/          # Business strategy
```

**Customize categories based on your niche.**

---

## ✅ What Goes Here

**Good content to add:**
- Industry infographics
- Data visualizations
- Framework diagrams
- Process flowcharts
- Comparison charts
- Timeline graphics
- Any visual content you can analyze/comment on

**File formats:**
- .jpg, .jpeg, .png (most common)
- .pdf (can work)
- High enough resolution for LinkedIn

---

## 🔍 Where to Find Content

**Create your own:**
- Canva
- Figma
- PowerPoint/Keynote

**Curate from others (with credits):**
- Industry reports
- Research papers
- Blog posts with visualizations
- Twitter/LinkedIn viral infographics
- Conference presentations (public)

**Always:**
- Track original author in `credits-EXAMPLE.json`
- Get permission if needed
- Give proper attribution

---

## 📝 Naming Convention

**Recommended format:**
```
[topic]-[short-description].jpg

Examples:
metrics-12-key-kpis.jpg
framework-product-market-fit.png
case-study-company-growth.jpg
```

---

## 🎯 How Many Do You Need?

**Minimum to start:** 10-15 infographics
**Comfortable:** 30-50 infographics
**Ideal:** 100+ infographics

**Why:**
- 1 infographic → 4-8 posts
- 30 infographics = 120-240 posts
- Enough for months of content

---

## 📋 Credits Tracking

**File:** `credits-EXAMPLE.json`

**Before publishing posts:**
1. Research original author
2. Update credits.json with info
3. Include credit in post (see `../02-templates/credits-template.md`)

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

1. Add 10-15 infographics to appropriate category folders
2. Update `credits-EXAMPLE.json` with authors
3. Generate your first batch using `../06-prompts/batch-generator.md`
